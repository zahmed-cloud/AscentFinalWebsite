# Ascent Content Guide

Read this before editing any copy on the site. It covers the voice, the hard rules, what is locked, and what is placeholder.

---

## 1. Voice

Plain, confident, operator-to-operator, lightly Aussie-inflected. Short sentences are good. Concrete beats clever. We sound like someone who does the work, not someone selling a deck.

**Banned fluff:** unlock, elevate, empower, supercharge, leverage (as a verb), game-changing, "engine" in the founder's About copy.

## 2. Hard rules

- **No em-dashes in body copy.** Use commas or full stops. The em-dash appears ONLY in UI chrome: the `EST. 2022 — USA / UK / UAE / AU / EU` eyebrow, section index labels (`01 — The process`), page titles, and the footer line.
- **No emojis.** Anywhere.
- **Never invent facts.** No made-up metrics, client names, quotes, or outcomes. Placeholders stay clearly marked until real proof replaces them.
- **Hyphenation:** "go-to-market" (noun form) everywhere.
- **Est. 2022** (not 2026).

## 3. Locked copy (do not change without the owner's say-so)

- Hero headline: **We're bloody good at growth**
- Tagline / subline: **Pipeline, authority and infrastructure for B2B.**
- Hero support: *We handle the whole go-to-market. We define the strategy, build the systems, and run them. One solid system, one point of accountability.*
- Eyebrows: `B2B GTM & OUTBOUND` (left) and `EST. 2022 — USA / UK / UAE / AU / EU` (right)
- Buttons: **Book a call** (sitewide), **See how it works** (hero)
- Nav labels (in order): Services · Industries · About · Case studies · Blog · Get in touch (Case studies lives at results.html)
- CTA band heading: **Let's get your pipeline moving.** on every page EXCEPT Services, which deliberately uses **Not sure where to start?**
- The six service names: GTM Strategy & Foundation · Outbound & Pipeline · Founder LinkedIn Ghostwriting · Paid Ads · Website, Landing Pages & CRO · Marketing Ops & Automation
- The three packages, all equal weight (no "most popular"): GTM Strategy & Foundation (Start here) · Pipeline & Authority (Core retainer) · Full Growth Partner (Everything)
- Founder block facts (About): Jamil Ahmed, **Founder & Growth Partner, Ascent**; leads a small focused team; no account managers between client and work; coverage USA / UK / UAE / Australia / Europe; operates globally from Dhaka with roots in Sydney. LinkedIn icon links to `linkedin.com/in/getascent`.
