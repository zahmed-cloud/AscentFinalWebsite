Needed
A predictable way to reach the people who hire. 360Partners, founded by Abbie Bowtell after a decade at Goldman Sachs and scaling 20VC, places exceptional full-time and fractional professionals into banks, investment firms, fintechs and VC-backed companies. How a system actually gets built

02 — The build, honestly

A proper outbound system is built, not switched on. The first month is research and infrastructure, because rushing warmup is how domains get burned and pipelines get poisoned. This is what the build really looks like.

Wk 1–2

Research and foundation

ICP, positioning, messaging and list building. Nothing sends yet.

Wk 2–4

Infrastructure and warmup

Domains, inboxes, authentication, deliverability warmup. This cannot be rushed.

Wk 4–6

Live and ramping

Sending starts low and climbs as deliverability holds. First replies land.

Wk 6–8

First real signal

Enough replies to read the market honestly and adjust.

Wk 8–10

Stable patterns

Data worth trusting. Targeting, message and channel mix get tuned.

Wk 10–14

Predictable pipeline

The system runs on numbers, not hope.

Once it is live, the ranges we run to

Operating ranges
2–6%

Reply rate

Depending on ICP and messaging.

25–35%

Replies that qualify

Real conversations, not polite brush-offs.

40–60%

Meeting show rate

Of qualified conversations booked.

Translated: from every 1,000 targeted prospects, expect 20 to 60 replies, 5 to 20 qualified conversations, and 3 to 10 booked meetings that actually happen. Ranges, not promises, and we measure all of it.
