From referral-dependent to an always-on outbound channel

360Partners, founded by Abbie Bowtell after a decade at Goldman Sachs and helping scale 20VC, places senior full-time and fractional professionals into banks, investment firms, fintechs and VC-backed companies across the UAE and UK.

Where they were stuck

Growth ran on Abbie's network. Strong months when introductions flowed, quiet ones when they did not. What changed

Outreach now goes out every week whether or not referrals show up, and conversations with the right founders and executives land on the calendar steadily. The new site holds up in front of an ex-Goldman network, and the engagement is ongoing.

Extremely proactive, hardworking, and always thinking outside the box. He has been a huge support across multiple areas of the company, particularly in business development and lead generation.— Abbie Bowtell · Founder, 360Partners

  • Founder-led LinkedIn outbound written in Abbie's voice, not agency-speak
  • Reply handling through to booked conversations
  • Candidate sourcing support on live mandates
  • The 360Partners website, designed and built end to end

How a system actually gets built

02 — The build, honestly

A proper outbound system is built, not switched on. The first month is research and infrastructure, because rushing warmup is how domains get burned and pipelines get poisoned. This is what the build really looks like.

Wk 1–2

Research and foundation

ICP, positioning, messaging and list building. Nothing sends yet.

Wk 2–4

Infrastructure and warmup

Domains, inboxes, authentication, deliverability warmup. This cannot be rushed.

Wk 4–6

Live and ramping

Sending starts low and climbs as deliverability holds. First replies land.

Wk 6–8

First real signal

Enough replies to read the market honestly and adjust.

Wk 8–10

Stable patterns

Data worth trusting. Targeting, message and channel mix get tuned.

Wk 10–14

Predictable pipeline

The system runs on numbers, not hope.
